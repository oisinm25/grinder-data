# Pedestal

### Built for the people who drive the boat.

The gun goes. The tack is called. Two people drop onto the pedestal and the next thirty
seconds decide whether the boat comes out of the turn in front or behind. Then they do it
again. And again. Forty times a race, three races a day, five days in a row.

That's grinding. It isn't strength, and it isn't fitness. It's both at once, on demand,
for a whole regatta.

**Pedestal is the training system built for it.**

---

## Three athletes in one body

A Grand Prix grinder has to be:

- **Strong**: heavy enough and strong enough to move serious load under a moving boat.
- **Explosive**: the 30-second, everything-you've-got grind off the start and out of every tack.
- **Relentless**: the aerobic engine to recover between efforts and repeat them, race after race.

Most training apps pick one. Pedestal trains all three, and keeps them balanced so one
never quietly eats the others.

## What it does

**🏋️ Strength that follows you, not a spreadsheet**
Every lift runs on its own progression rule. Next week's weight comes from what you
actually lifted this week, with the reason written next to it:
*"hit 100 × 6 @ RPE 8 → 102.5 kg"*.

**⚙️ Grinding, measured properly**
Heart-rate load scores undercount short upper-body efforts, so Pedestal tracks grinding
the way it actually loads you: effort count, work minutes, and time above threshold.

**🟢🟡🔴 Readiness that explains itself**
HRV, resting heart rate and sleep against your own baseline give a green, amber or red
day, plus the reason. On an amber day the weights hold. On a red day they come down. You
always know why.

**🚴 Sessions straight to your watch**
Bike and erg sessions land on your Garmin and in Zwift with your watt targets already
set. You press start.

**📊 An honest Sunday**
Every session graded against the plan: held, faded or missed. Progress against your goal
lines, and a clear flag when something has to give.

**🌍 Built for a sailing calendar**
Regattas, delivery trips, light-air days that leave you fresh and heavy-air days that
empty you. Pedestal bends the week around the season, without losing sight of the goal.

## How it works

```
   You plan it          Pedestal runs it            You see it
 ───────────────     ──────────────────────     ──────────────────
  goals, blocks  →   reads your data, sets   →   morning summary,
  and rules          today's weights and         sessions on your
                     watts, grades what          watch, Sunday
                     you did                     report
```

The plan always stays in your hands, or your coach's. Pedestal runs it, measures it and
reports back. It never quietly rewrites it.

## Who it's for

- **Grinders, pit and trimming crew** in TP52, Grand Prix, SailGP-style and America's Cup programmes
- **Strength and conditioning coaches** running a squad of sailors through a season
- **Ambitious amateur crew** who want proper, structured training that fits around racing

## Why it exists

Pedestal was built by a professional TP52 grinder who couldn't find a training system that
understood the job. It started as his own winter programme, and grew into the tool he wished
he'd had all along.

> *The training system a professional grinder built for himself.*
