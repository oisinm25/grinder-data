Race-day pacing: IF 0.65–0.70 (≈240–260 W NP at 370 W FTP), no surges above ~330 W.

## Grinding progression

- Every upper session opens with the explosive bench pull (best predictor of grinding output).
- Hand-bike sprints on fresh upper-body days; 4-min work on fatigue-tolerant days.
- Oct–Dec: confirm hand-bike FTP, build aerobic 4-min work.
- Jan–Feb: VO2-type 3–4 min intervals, heavier bench pull.
- Mar–Apr: race-simulation repeat sprints, held while ride volume peaks.
- If 4-min hand-bike power drops two weeks running, cut the bench variation day first.
- Bike VO2 and hand-bike VO2 sessions at least 48 h apart.
